This browser prototype supports the listed conversions. Legacy DOC, Excel, PowerPoint, scanned PDF OCR, and advanced PDF-to-Word layout conversion require additional processing or specialized libraries and are not included yet. Files are processed locally in your browser where possible. Internet is required to load conversion engines on first use.
